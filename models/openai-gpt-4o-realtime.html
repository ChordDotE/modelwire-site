gpt-4o-realtime

Is gpt-4o-realtime deprecated, and when does it stop working?

gpt-4o-realtime is scheduled to shut down on 2027-01-20. openai lists it as Deprecated on its own deprecation page. That is 141 days from 2026-09-01. Recommended replacement: gpt-realtime-2.1.
