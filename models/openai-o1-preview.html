o1-preview

Is o1-preview deprecated, and when does it stop working?

o1-preview passed the shutdown date openai published for it, 2025-07-28 (391 days ago). We record the date the provider published; we do not call the endpoint to check. Recommended replacement: o3.

Facts on this page come from the append-only ledger; last change recorded 2026-08-23. Primary source is linked at the bottom of the page.

What should o1-preview be replaced with?

openai names openai/o3 as the replacement. The comparison below uses our own last reading of the public catalog (2026-08-23), in USD per 1M tokens.

This model is no longer in the public catalog we read, so there is no current price of its own to compare against. The replacement's values are shown alone.
