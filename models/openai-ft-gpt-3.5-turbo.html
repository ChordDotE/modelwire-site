ft-gpt-3.5-turbo

Is ft-gpt-3.5-turbo deprecated, and when does it stop working?

ft-gpt-3.5-turbo is scheduled to shut down on 2026-10-23. openai lists it as Deprecated on its own deprecation page. That is 69 days from 2026-08-15. Recommended replacement: gpt-5.6-terra.

Facts on this page come from the append-only ledger; last change recorded 2026-08-14. Primary source is linked at the bottom of the page.

openai names openai/gpt-5.6-terra as the replacement. The comparison below uses our own last reading of the public catalog (2026-08-14), in USD per 1M tokens.
